# Prompts Oficiales para Prototipado en Google Stitch (stitch.withgoogle.com)

Utiliza estos prompts estructurados en **Google Stitch** para generar pantallas de alta fidelidad, flujos móviles y variantes de diseño complementarias para la reunión con el CROE y el Círculo Militar del Ecuador.

---

## 1. Portal Oficial & Carné Militar 3D (Círculo Militar del Ecuador)

```text
Design a high-fidelity, executive-grade mobile and desktop web application for 'Círculo Militar del Ecuador' (est. 1918, prestigious armed forces officers club).
Aesthetic: Deep navy blue (#1B365D), rich metallic gold (#C8A051), slate-900 accents, clean glassmorphism panels with 16px background blur, subtle inner borders, and weightless floating cards.
Screens to include:
1. Executive Hero with circular heraldic crest, institutional motto, quick stats (6,038 active members, 108 years tradition).
2. 3D Digital Officer ID Credential: Front with EMV chip, military rank badge (Coronel de E.M.), anti-screenshot holographic pulse, dynamic cryptographic QR code (SHA-256), and biometric photo. Back side with magnetic stripe and emergency military contacts.
3. Military Checkpoint (Garita) Scanner interface with laser HUD, real-time access authorization status (VERIFICADO - ACCESO CONCEDIDO), and dependent gate-pass registry.
Typography: Serif headers for military solemnity, clean geometric sans-serif for UI data.
```

## 2. Portal Oficial & Carné Militar (CROE - Club de Recreación de Oficiales del Ejército)

```text
Design an executive officer portal for 'Club de Recreación de Oficiales del Ejército - CROE'.
Aesthetic: Deep military burgundy/wine (#8B1E24), warm golden accents (#D4AF37), forest green subtle trims, elegant dark slate backgrounds, and glassmorphic card overlays.
Screens to include:
1. Institutional Dashboard with CROE rectangular crest (golden 5-point star, diagonal red/green split, motto 'EL PLACER DE SERVIR').
2. Digital Member Dossier: Active officer credential, family dependents list with security QR codes for pool and tennis court access.
3. Multi-Tenant Switcher bar at top allowing one-click transition between CROE and Círculo Militar with live theme adaptation.
```

## 3. Comedor Militar por Turnos & Banquetes con Explosión de Insumos

```text
Design an institutional dining and banquet management interface for a military officer club.
Style: Dark mode / luxury slate dashboard with vibrant amber and emerald status indicators.
Features:
1. Shift Selector: Military Breakfast (06:30-09:30), Executive Lunch (12:00-15:00), Officers Dinner (18:30-21:30), and Institutional Banquet (PAX slider from 10 to 350+ guests).
2. Bill of Materials (BoM) real-time calculation card: Displays immediate kitchen demand as guest count changes (e.g., 200 PAX = 200 cloth napkins, 200 cutlery sets, 400 sodas, 50kg beef tenderloin).
3. Post-Event Tableware Audit modal: Checkbox and counter for returned crystal glasses, porcelain plates, and silver cutlery with automatic loss/breakage penalty calculations.
```

## 4. Economato, Kardex Inmutable & Carga de Facturas SRI

```text
Design an advanced warehouse inventory (Economato) and Kardex tracking dashboard for military facilities.
Aesthetic: Financial and logistics terminal, clean tabular layout, color-coded stock level bars (normal green, reorder amber, critical red).
Features:
1. 4 Categorized Warehouses: Disposables & Napkins, Cutlery & Tableware, Beverages & Sodas, Kitchen Food & Proteins.
2. Electronic Supplier Invoice Dropzone (SRI Ecuador): Drag-and-drop zone with instant preset buttons for Ecuadorian suppliers (PRONACA S.A., Tesalia Springs Güitig, Indurmesa).
3. Invoice Reading Preview: Displays 13-digit RUC, official 49-digit SRI Electronic Access Key, 15% IVA tax breakdown, and 'Confirm Ingestion' button that instantly bumps warehouse stock and writes an IN_PURCHASE entry to the immutable Kardex ledger.
```
